# Compensation and Payroll

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Northwind Robotics is an invented company.
> Nothing in this file is real employment advice or a real company policy.

## Pay Dates and Payslips

Salaried employees are paid **monthly, on the 25th of each month**. If the 25th
falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the last working day
before it.

Payslips are published in Workday **two business days before** the pay date.
Payroll queries go to `payroll@northwind-robotics.example` and are answered
within 3 business days.

## The Compensation Review Cycle

Northwind runs **one compensation review per year, effective 1 April**.

The cycle timeline:

- **January** — managers submit proposed adjustments.
- **February** — calibration across departments.
- **March** — outcomes communicated to employees individually.
- **1 April** — new salaries take effect, reflected in the April payslip.

Compensation review is **separate from the performance review cycle** and
separate from the promotion cycle. A strong performance review does not
automatically produce a salary increase, and a salary increase is not a
promotion.

Employees hired **after 1 October** are not included in the following April
cycle; their first review is the April after that.

## The Promotion Cycle

Promotions are considered **twice per year**, effective **1 April** and
**1 October**.

Promotion requires a written case from the employee's manager, evidence of
sustained performance at the next level, and approval at a cross-department
calibration meeting. Time in level is not by itself a qualification.

A promotion carries a salary adjustment to at least the minimum of the new
level's band. This adjustment is applied on the promotion's effective date and
is independent of the annual April compensation review.

## Annual Bonus

Employees at L3 and above are eligible for an annual bonus, expressed as a
percentage of base salary and paid in the **March payslip** each year.

Target bonus by level:

| Job level | Target bonus |
| --------- | ------------ |
| L3        | 5%           |
| L4        | 10%          |
| L5        | 15%          |
| L6        | 20%          |
| L7        | 25%          |

The actual payout is the target multiplied by a company performance factor
(0 to 1.5) set by the board each January. Employees must be employed on the
payment date to receive the bonus. Employees who joined during the bonus year
receive a pro-rated amount based on completed months of service.

## Equity

Employees at L3 and above receive a stock option grant on joining. Grants vest
over **four years with a one-year cliff**: 25% vests on the first anniversary of
the vesting start date, and the remainder vests **monthly** over the following
36 months.

Refresh grants are considered as part of the annual April compensation review.
Full details of exercise windows and tax treatment are in your individual grant
agreement, which takes precedence over this handbook.

## Salary Bands and Pay Transparency

Northwind publishes internal salary bands for every level and function on the
People wiki. Any employee may view any band.

Employees are free to discuss their own compensation with colleagues. Northwind
will not discipline anyone for doing so, and any contract clause that appears to
forbid it is void and unenforceable at Northwind.

## Referral Bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and completes probation receive a
referral bonus of **2,000 USD** (or local equivalent), paid in the payslip
following the referred employee's probation completion.

The referral must be logged in the applicant tracking system before the
candidate's first interview. Hiring managers and recruiters are not eligible for
referral bonuses on roles they are hiring for.
