# Time Off and Leave

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Northwind Robotics is an invented company.
> Nothing in this file is real employment advice or a real company policy.

Northwind Robotics offers several distinct kinds of leave. They accrue
differently, are approved differently, and are paid differently. Read the
section that matches your situation — do not assume the rules carry across.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Employees accrue **25 days of PTO per calendar year**, accruing monthly at 2.083
days per completed month of service. PTO covers vacation and personal days.

- PTO requests go through Workday and require manager approval.
- Requests of **5 consecutive days or more** must be submitted at least
  **21 days in advance**.
- Requests of fewer than 5 days should be submitted at least 3 days in advance
  where possible.
- You may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar
  year. Carried-over days expire on **31 March**.
- PTO does not need a stated reason. Managers may decline a specific date for
  business reasons but may not ask why you are taking it.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is **separate from PTO and does not come out of your PTO balance**.

Employees receive **10 paid sick days per calendar year**. Sick leave does not
carry over and is not paid out on termination.

- For absences of **1–2 consecutive days**, notify your manager by message
  before the start of your working day. No documentation is required.
- For absences of **3 or more consecutive days**, a medical certificate must be
  submitted to HR within 5 business days of returning.
- If sick leave is exhausted, further medical absence is handled under Extended
  Medical Leave (below), not under PTO.

## Extended Medical Leave

Employees who have completed probation and who exhaust their 10 paid sick days
may apply for **Extended Medical Leave of up to 12 weeks** in a rolling 12-month
period.

Extended Medical Leave is paid at **70% of base salary** for the first 8 weeks
and is unpaid thereafter. It requires a medical certificate and approval from an
HR business partner. Benefits continue in full for the whole period.

## Parental Leave

Parental leave is available to all new parents regardless of gender, and applies
to birth, adoption, and long-term foster placement.

- Employees with **at least 12 months of service** receive **16 weeks of parental
  leave at 100% of base salary**.
- Employees with **less than 12 months of service** receive **8 weeks at 100% of
  base salary**.
- Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement.
- It may be taken in up to **three separate blocks**, each at least 2 weeks long.
- Notify HR at least **30 days before** your intended start date where possible.

Parental leave does not reduce your PTO balance and continues to accrue PTO
normally.

## Bereavement Leave

Bereavement leave is fully paid, does not come out of PTO, and does not require
documentation.

- **Immediate family** (partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or
  equivalent): **10 days**.
- **Extended family or a close personal relationship**: **3 days**.
- Additional unpaid time may be arranged with your manager.

Notify your manager directly. A Workday entry can be filed afterwards; no one
should be filling in forms during a bereavement.

## Unpaid Personal Leave

After 12 months of service, employees may request **unpaid personal leave of up
to 3 months** for reasons not covered by the categories above — for example
study, extended travel, or personal projects.

Unpaid personal leave requires approval from your manager and a department head,
and should be requested at least **60 days in advance**. PTO does not accrue
during unpaid personal leave. Health benefits continue, but the employee is
responsible for the premium share normally covered by payroll deduction.

## Public Holidays

Employees observe the public holidays of their registered work country. In
addition, Northwind closes company-wide for the final week of December. This
closure does not count against PTO.

Employees may swap up to **3 public holidays** per year for days of personal or
religious significance, by notifying their manager at least 14 days ahead.
