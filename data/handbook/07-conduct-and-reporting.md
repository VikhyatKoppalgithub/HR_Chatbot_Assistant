# Code of Conduct and Reporting

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Northwind Robotics is an invented company.
> Nothing in this file is real employment advice or a real company policy.

## Our Standard

Northwind Robotics expects everyone to treat colleagues, customers, candidates,
and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation
are grounds for dismissal.

This applies everywhere work happens: offices, video calls, chat, email,
customer sites, conferences, and work social events.

## Reporting a Concern

There are four routes for raising a concern. You may use any of them, in any
order, and you do not need to try one before another.

1. **Your manager** — appropriate for most day-to-day concerns.
2. **Any HR business partner** — directly, without involving your manager.
3. **The confidential reporting line** at
   `speakup@northwind-robotics.example`, monitored by two named senior HR staff.
4. **The independent external hotline**, operated by a third party. Reports here
   may be made anonymously. Details are on the People wiki and on posters in
   every office.

You never have to report a concern to the person it is about, or to anyone in
that person's reporting line.

## What Happens After a Report

HR acknowledges every report **within 2 business days**.

For matters requiring investigation, an investigator with no involvement in the
matter is appointed within 5 business days. The person who raised the concern is
told the outcome, and the general nature of any action taken, within 30 days
where possible. If an investigation will take longer, they are told why.

Both the person raising a concern and the person it concerns may bring a
colleague or a support person to any meeting.

## Retaliation

Retaliation against someone for raising a concern in good faith, or for taking
part in an investigation, is itself a dismissible offence — independent of
whether the original concern was upheld.

If you believe you are experiencing retaliation, report it through any of the
four routes above. Retaliation reports are escalated to a department head
immediately.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must declare anything that could reasonably look like a conflict of
interest, including:

- A close personal or family relationship with a colleague, particularly where
  one reports to the other, directly or indirectly.
- A financial interest in a customer, supplier, competitor, or partner beyond a
  holding in a broad-market fund.
- A relationship with a candidate you would be involved in hiring.
- Any outside role that touches Northwind's market.

Declarations go to `conduct@northwind-robotics.example` within **14 days** of
the situation arising. Declaring a conflict is not an admission of wrongdoing —
almost all declared conflicts are simply managed, usually by moving one person
out of a decision.

## Relationships at Work

Consensual relationships between colleagues are permitted and are not anyone
else's business, with one exception: a relationship between two people where one
has any influence over the other's pay, promotion, performance rating, or
continued employment must be declared so that reporting lines can be adjusted.

The declaration is confidential and is handled by an HR business partner.

## Outside Work and Moonlighting

Employees may hold outside employment, run a business, freelance, advise
startups, contribute to open source, teach, write, and speak — provided that the
outside work:

- does not compete with Northwind Robotics,
- does not use Northwind confidential information, equipment, or work time,
- does not create a conflict of interest, and
- does not prevent the employee from meeting their responsibilities.

Outside roles in the robotics, industrial automation, or warehouse-logistics
sectors must be declared and approved in advance. Everything else needs no
approval, though telling your manager is encouraged.

## Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose Northwind confidential information during or after
employment. This includes unreleased product plans, customer lists, pricing,
source code, security details, and personal data of colleagues.

Nothing in this policy prevents an employee from:

- discussing their own pay and working conditions,
- reporting suspected illegal conduct to a regulator or law enforcement,
- responding truthfully to a lawful order, or
- raising a concern under this policy.

## Use of AI Tools

Approved AI assistants may be used for day-to-day work. Employees must not paste
customer personal data, unreleased product information, credentials, or source
code from private repositories into unapproved external AI tools.

The current list of approved tools is on the Security wiki. Output from an AI
tool is the employee's responsibility — check it before you ship it or send it.

## Substances

Northwind does not test employees for drugs. Alcohol is available at some
company events and there is never any expectation to drink. Being impaired at
work to the point of being unable to work safely, particularly in a lab or
workshop, is a safety matter and is handled as one.
