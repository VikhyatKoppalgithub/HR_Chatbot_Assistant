# Employment Basics

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Northwind Robotics is an invented company.
> Nothing in this file is real employment advice or a real company policy.

## Employment Status and Probation

All new employees at Northwind Robotics serve a **probation period of 90 calendar
days** from their start date. During probation, either party may end the
employment relationship with **7 days' written notice**.

Probation may be extended once, by up to 30 additional days, if the employee's
manager and an HR business partner both agree in writing. The employee must be
told of any extension before day 85.

Employees who complete probation successfully receive written confirmation and
become eligible for the full benefits package described in the Benefits and
Stipends policy.

## Notice Periods After Probation

Once probation is complete, the required notice period depends on job level:

| Job level | Notice period (either party) |
| --------- | ---------------------------- |
| L1 – L3   | 30 days                      |
| L4 – L5   | 60 days                      |
| L6 and above | 90 days                   |

Notice must be given in writing to both the employee's manager and
`people@northwind-robotics.example`. Northwind may elect to pay salary in lieu
of notice. Unused accrued PTO is paid out with the final paycheck.

## Job Levels and Titles

Northwind Robotics uses a seven-level ladder shared across engineering, design,
operations, and go-to-market functions.

- **L1 – L2** — Associate. Works on well-defined tasks with regular guidance.
- **L3** — Professional. Owns defined problems end to end.
- **L4** — Senior. Owns ambiguous problems and mentors others.
- **L5** — Staff / Lead. Sets direction for a team or workstream.
- **L6** — Principal / Manager of managers. Sets direction across teams.
- **L7** — Distinguished / Director. Sets direction across the company.

Levels are internal and are not always reflected in external job titles.

## Standard Working Hours

The standard work week is **40 hours across Monday to Friday**. Northwind does
not track hours worked for salaried employees; we track outcomes.

Employees are expected to be reachable during **core hours of 11:00–16:00 in
their registered home time zone**. Outside core hours, asynchronous
communication is the default and no one is expected to reply immediately.

Overtime pay applies only to employees classified as non-exempt under local law.
Non-exempt employees must record overtime in Workday before the end of the pay
period in which it was worked.

## Employment Records and Personal Details

Employees are responsible for keeping their personal details current in Workday,
including home address, registered work time zone, emergency contact, and bank
details for payroll.

Changes to bank details must be submitted at least **10 business days before**
the next pay date, or they will take effect the following month. Bank detail
changes always trigger a confirmation email plus a phone verification call from
Payroll — Northwind will never action a change from email alone.
