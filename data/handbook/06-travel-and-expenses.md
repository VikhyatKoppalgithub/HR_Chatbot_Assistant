# Travel and Expenses

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Northwind Robotics is an invented company.
> Nothing in this file is real employment advice or a real company policy.

## General Principles

Spend Northwind's money as you would your own. Book in advance where you can,
choose the reasonable option rather than the cheapest miserable one, and keep
receipts.

All expenses are submitted through **Expensify within 60 days** of being
incurred. Claims older than 60 days require a written exception from a
department head and may be declined.

Reimbursements are paid with the **next monthly payroll run** for claims
approved at least 5 business days before the pay date.

## Approval Thresholds

Approval requirements are based on the total cost of the trip or purchase, not
the individual line item.

| Total cost           | Approval required                    |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Under 500 USD        | None — submit the claim directly     |
| 500 – 2,000 USD      | Manager approval before booking      |
| 2,001 – 10,000 USD   | Department head approval before booking |
| Over 10,000 USD      | Finance and department head approval |

Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is a disciplinary matter.

## Flights

- Flights under 6 hours: **economy class**.
- Flights of 6 hours or more: **premium economy**.
- Flights of 10 hours or more: **business class**, with department head approval.

Book at least **14 days in advance** where possible. If a fare within 10% of the
lowest available is more convenient — better timing, fewer connections, a
preferred airline for status — take it; do not spend an hour saving 30 USD.

Employees may keep personal airline and hotel loyalty points earned on business
travel.

## Hotels

Nightly hotel caps, excluding tax:

| Location tier | Nightly cap |
| ------------- | ----------- |
| Tier 1 cities (London, New York, San Francisco, Tokyo, Singapore) | 350 USD |
| Tier 2 cities | 250 USD |
| All other locations | 180 USD |

If no room is available within the cap, book the closest reasonable option and
note the reason in the claim. Exceeding the cap with a stated reason is fine;
exceeding it silently is not.

Northwind does not reimburse hotel minibar, room-service alcohol, in-room movies,
or spa charges.

## Meals

Daily meal caps while travelling, including tax and tip:

| Location tier | Daily meal cap |
| ------------- | -------------- |
| Tier 1 cities | 90 USD         |
| Tier 2 cities | 70 USD         |
| All other locations | 50 USD   |

The cap is a daily total, not per meal. Alcohol is reimbursable up to **2 drinks
per person per day** when part of a meal.

Meals with customers or candidates are not covered by the daily cap; claim them
as client entertainment and note the attendees.

## Ground Transport

Standard-tier ride-hailing, taxis, and public transport are reimbursable.
Premium ride tiers are reimbursable only between 22:00 and 06:00, or when
carrying equipment.

Personal car mileage is reimbursed at the prevailing statutory rate for your
work country. Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts. Fines and
penalties are never reimbursable.

Rental cars require manager approval and should be mid-size or smaller unless
three or more colleagues are travelling together.

## Team Events and Offsites

Each team receives a social budget of **150 USD per person per quarter**. It
does not carry over between quarters.

Department offsites require department head approval and a written agenda.
Offsite venues must be accessible, and every offsite must include at least one
option for employees who do not drink alcohol.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursable: traffic and parking fines, personal travel
insurance beyond the company policy, flight upgrades paid personally then
claimed, in-flight gambling or entertainment purchases, personal grooming,
and any expense without a receipt where a receipt was obtainable.
