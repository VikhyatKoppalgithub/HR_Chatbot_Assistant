# Benefits and Stipends

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Northwind Robotics is an invented company.
> Nothing in this file is real employment advice or a real company policy.

Northwind Robotics offers **three separate annual stipends**. They have
different amounts, different rules, and different approval paths. They cannot be
combined or transferred between categories.

## Home Office Stipend

Every employee receives a **one-time home office setup stipend of 1,500 USD** on
joining, plus a **refresh of 500 USD every two years** thereafter.

Eligible purchases include a desk, an office chair, a monitor, a keyboard and
mouse, a desk lamp, and noise-cancelling headphones. Ergonomic equipment such as
a sit-stand desk or a supportive chair is explicitly encouraged.

Not eligible: consumables, decorative items, smart-home devices, furniture for
rooms other than the workspace, and any item primarily for personal use.

Equipment bought with the home office stipend belongs to the employee, not to
Northwind, and does not need to be returned when leaving.

Claims are submitted through Expensify with a receipt. No manager pre-approval
is needed if the item is on the eligible list.

## Wellness Stipend

Every employee receives a **wellness stipend of 900 USD per calendar year**,
claimable monthly or in a lump sum.

Eligible: gym and studio memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment, therapy
and counselling sessions, meditation and sleep apps, nutrition consultations,
and childcare during working hours.

The wellness stipend does not carry over — unused amounts expire on 31 December.
No manager approval is required. Receipts are required for claims over 50 USD.

Therapy and counselling claims are processed by an external administrator and
are **never visible to your manager or to Northwind HR** in itemised form.

## Learning and Development Stipend

Every employee receives a **learning stipend of 2,000 USD per calendar year** for
professional development.

Eligible: conference tickets and travel, online courses, professional
certifications, books, coaching, and language lessons relevant to your role.

- Claims **under 500 USD** need no pre-approval.
- Claims **of 500 USD or more** require written manager approval before purchase.
- Up to **1,000 USD** of unused learning stipend may be carried into the next
  calendar year; the carried amount expires 30 June.

Northwind also grants **5 learning days per year** — paid days for study,
courses, or conferences. Learning days are separate from PTO and do not reduce
your PTO balance. They do not carry over.

If Northwind pays for a certification costing more than 3,000 USD and the
employee leaves within 12 months, a pro-rated repayment may be required. This is
agreed in writing before the purchase, never applied retroactively.

## Health Insurance

Northwind provides private medical insurance for all employees from **day one**
— there is no probation-related waiting period. Cover includes inpatient,
outpatient, mental health, and dental.

Employees may add a partner and dependent children. The company pays 100% of the
employee premium and 50% of dependant premiums; the remainder is deducted from
payroll.

Changes to cover can be made within 30 days of joining, during the annual
enrolment window each November, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event
(marriage, birth, adoption, or a partner's loss of cover).

## Retirement and Pension

Northwind matches employee retirement contributions up to **6% of base salary**.
Matching begins from the first full month of employment and is not subject to
probation.

Employees are automatically enrolled at a 4% contribution rate and may change or
opt out at any time in Workday. Employer matching contributions vest immediately.

## Life and Income Protection

All employees receive life cover at **4x base salary** and income protection that
pays **60% of base salary** after a 13-week absence, continuing to age 65 or
recovery. Both are provided at no cost to the employee and require no medical
underwriting below the scheme's automatic acceptance limit.
