# Remote and Hybrid Work

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA. Northwind Robotics is an invented company.
> Nothing in this file is real employment advice or a real company policy.

## Working Arrangement Types

Northwind Robotics supports three working arrangements. Your arrangement is
recorded in Workday and is agreed with your manager.

- **Office-based** — you work from a Northwind office at least 4 days per week.
- **Hybrid** — you work from a Northwind office **at least 2 days per week**,
  and remotely for the rest.
- **Fully remote** — you have no regular office attendance requirement.

Hybrid is the default for employees who live within 50 km of an office. Fully
remote is the default for everyone else.

## Changing Your Arrangement

Requests to change arrangement are made in Workday and require manager approval.
Managers should respond within 10 business days and must give a written reason
if declining.

A declined request can be escalated to a department head, whose decision is
final for 6 months.

## Office Days and Anchor Days

Teams choose their own **anchor days** — the days when hybrid team members are
in the office together. Anchor days are set per team, published on the team
wiki, and reviewed each quarter.

Hybrid employees should attend on their team's anchor days. If an anchor day is
impossible in a given week, tell your team in advance; there is no approval
process and no penalty.

## Working From Another Country

Employees may work from a country other than their registered work country for
up to **30 calendar days per rolling 12-month period** without a formal change
of employment terms.

Requests must be submitted at least **14 days in advance** and are reviewed by
People Operations for tax and immigration implications. Some countries are
restricted for legal reasons; the current list is on the People wiki.

Working from another country for **more than 30 days** requires a formal
relocation, described below. Do not extend a temporary stay without approval —
it creates personal tax exposure for you as well as for Northwind.

## Permanent Relocation

Employees may request permanent relocation to any country where Northwind has a
legal entity. The current entity list is on the People wiki.

Relocation requires **90 days' notice** and approval from your manager and
People Operations. Salary is re-set to the band for the new location, which may
mean an increase or a decrease. Northwind contributes up to **5,000 USD** toward
documented relocation costs, paid on production of receipts.

Relocation to a country where Northwind has no legal entity is not possible.

## Co-Working Spaces

Fully remote employees who do not live within 50 km of a Northwind office may
claim a co-working space membership of up to **250 USD per month**.

This is claimed monthly through Expensify with a receipt and requires one-time
manager approval, after which it renews automatically. It is separate from, and
does not affect, the home office stipend.

## Core Hours and Availability

Regardless of arrangement, employees are expected to be reachable during **core
hours of 11:00–16:00 in their registered work time zone**.

Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours. Recurring meetings that fall
outside core hours for any attendee should be rotated so the burden is shared.

Employees who need to shift their working day — for caring responsibilities, for
example — can agree adjusted core hours with their manager and record them in
Workday.

## Equipment and Security for Remote Work

Northwind provides a laptop to every employee. This is company property and must
be returned on leaving. It is **not** part of the home office stipend, which
covers furniture and peripherals only.

Remote workers must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from
public networks, keep full-disk encryption enabled, and report a lost or stolen
device to `security@northwind-robotics.example` within 24 hours.

Personal devices may not be used to access customer data under any circumstances.
