The finding: hybrid search — the conventional default — scored worse than plain dense retrieval on MRR, because fusing in BM25's weaker ranking pushed correct passages down. Reranking then reached perfect recall. Without an evaluation harness, hybrid would have shipped as "the better system".

Try “Can I expense a gym membership?” and compare: bm25 ranks Non-Reimbursable Expenses first — that section is full of the words “expense” and “reimbursable”, so it looks lexically perfect while being about what you cannot claim.
